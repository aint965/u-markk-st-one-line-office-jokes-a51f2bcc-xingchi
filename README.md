A single-line joke about office life for the [One-Line Office Jokes](https://make.fun/bounty/u-markk-st-one-line-office-jokes-a51f2bcc) competition on make.fun.

The program writes one joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Nothing travels faster in our office than the reply-all apologizing for the reply-all.

The laugh lands in the last few words: the apology for the reply-all mistake was itself sent by reply-all, so the very thing everyone is apologizing for keeps happening — the fastest email in the office is the one saying sorry for the last one.
